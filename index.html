About me

Last updated: February 2026

I am a recent graduate from the University of Illinois (UIUC), where I got my bachelor's degree in computer science. My interests include robotics, deep learning, math, and software engineering. My goal for the next decade is to make robots ubiquitous in our everyday lives. I've recently joined Mind Robotics as an early researcher 🚀.

Last fall, I worked on frontier VLAs at Dyna Robotics as a research intern. Over the summer, I was writing robotics infrastructure in Rust for Anduril Industries. Previous experiences also include full-stack development at Capital One and LLM finetuning at a startup.

At UIUC, I was the founder and a co-chair for SIGRobotics, a special interest group under ACM@UIUC dedicated to encouraging project-based exploration in robotics. Starting and growing this club was my greatest achievement in college. It now boasts over 40 active members, 8-10 projects, and amazing sponsors such as Hugging Face, Saronic, and Neuralink.

Feel free to contact me via email (advaitpatel04 at gmail dot com) or through any of the following links: X, LinkedIn, GitHub, Resume.

Past work

  • Various robotics projects through SIGRobotics and on my own time, such as training robotic arms to fold laundry.
